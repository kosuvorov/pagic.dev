Questions

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, and browsers built on Chromium 127 or newer, like Edge, Brave, Arc and Helium.

Will other people see my changes?

No. Pagic only changes how a site looks in your own browser. The site itself, and what everyone else sees, stays the same.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. You click things and pick colors. If you do know CSS, you can switch on Code in Pagic's settings and add your own.

Can I get the original site back?

Always. Every change is listed in Pagic, with a switch to turn it off or a button to remove it, and ⌘Z undoes the last one. The big switch at the top turns Pagic off for that site. Your presets stay saved either way.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free has every tool, on one site at a time, and Reader everywhere. Pro customizes every site at once and adds presets: several looks per site, looks for some pages or for dark mode, and sharing them as files.

Does it work on every site?

On regular websites, yes. Browsers don't let any extension change their own pages, like the New Tab page, settings, or the Web Store. When a site redesigns itself, a change may need a quick touch-up.

What can Pagic see?

Only the sites you let it customize, and it asks for each one. Everything it saves stays in your browser. See the privacy policy.

What's the AI helper?

Turn on Ask in Pagic's settings, describe a change ("make the comments smaller"), and Claude or ChatGPT makes it as a draft you keep or throw away. It's optional, uses your own API key, and only sends anything when you ask.

Can I share my look with a friend?

Yes, with Pro. Choose Export as a file in the preset menu, and they add it with Import from a file. Its pictures and fonts go along.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your Polar customer portal, any time. See support for help with licenses.
