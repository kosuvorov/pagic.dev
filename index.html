Questions

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, and browsers built on Chromium 127 or newer, like Edge, Brave, Arc and Helium.

Will other people see my changes?

No. Pagic only changes how a site looks in your own browser. The site itself, and what everyone else sees, stays the same.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. You click things and pick colors. If you do know CSS, Advanced mode lets you add your own.

Can I get the original site back?

Always. Hold the eye button to peek at the original, undo any single change, or switch Pagic off for that site. Your presets stay saved either way.

Does it work on every site?

On regular websites, yes. Browsers don't let any extension change their own pages, like the New Tab page, settings, or the Web Store. When a site redesigns itself, an edit may need a quick touch-up.

What can Pagic see?

Only the sites you let it customize, and it asks for each one. Everything it saves stays in your browser. See the privacy policy.

What's the AI helper?

In Advanced mode you can describe a change ("make the comments smaller") and let Claude or ChatGPT make it. It's optional, uses your own API key, and only sends anything when you ask.

Can I share my look with a friend?

Yes. Save a preset as a file, with its pictures and fonts, and they can add it from Pagic's settings.
