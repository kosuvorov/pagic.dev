About Pagic

Pagic is a Chrome extension for making any website your own: hide what you don't need, pick your colors and fonts, highlight and leave notes, and keep it that way on every visit.

Who makes it

I'm Konstantin Suvorov, an indie developer. I build Pagic on my own, the same way I build VoicePrompter, a voice-controlled teleprompter app.

I use Pagic every day myself. Hacker News is dark, Wikipedia is set in New York, and my recipe sites are covered in sticky notes.

Elsewhere: konsu.dev · YouTube · LinkedIn · GitHub

How Pagic treats your data

There's no account. Your presets, notes, pictures and fonts are saved in your browser. Pagic starts with access to no websites and asks for each site you choose to customize. The optional AI helper uses your own API key and only sends anything when you ask it to. The details are in the privacy policy.

Pagic in one paragraph

Pagic works in Chrome and other browsers built on Chromium 127 or newer, like Edge, Brave, Arc and Helium. It's free for one site at a time, with every tool, and Reader is free on every site. Pro customizes every site and adds presets. If Pro ends, nothing you made is deleted.
