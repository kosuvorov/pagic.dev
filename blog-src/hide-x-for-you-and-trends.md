# How to Hide Trends, Who to Follow and For You on X (Twitter)

To hide trends, "Who to follow" and the For You tab on X in a desktop browser, use an extension that hides parts of the page and remembers it, since X has no setting to remove them. In Pagic you click each panel and choose **Hide**, once, and X opens without them on every visit. You can keep the Following timeline and hide everything around it.

## What X lets you change in its settings

A little:

- **Explore settings** let you turn off trends based on your location and interests, but the "What's happening" panel still shows trends.
- **Content you see** lets you mute words and accounts.
- The **Following** tab shows only accounts you follow.

There's no off switch for the trends panel, "Who to follow", Premium prompts or the For You tab.

## How to clean up X with Pagic

Open x.com, click Pagic's icon and **Customize this site**. Click the eraser to start hiding, then:

1. **What's happening.** Point at the trends box in the right column, press <kbd>↑</kbd> until the whole box is outlined, and click.
2. **Who to follow.** Same, in the right column. If suggestion blocks also appear inside your timeline, <kbd>Alt</kbd>-click one to hide all like it.
3. **Premium and subscribe prompts**, wherever they show up.
4. **The For You tab.** Click Following first, then hide the For You tab at the top of the timeline so you don't switch back by accident.
5. **The search box in the right column**, if you only search from Explore.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> when you're done. The right column is now empty or gone, and the timeline is the only thing left.

## Can I make the timeline wider once the sidebar is gone?

Often, yes. With the right column hidden, use **Edit page → Size** to drag the timeline's edge wider, or the Text panel's **text width** slider for longer lines in posts. How far X's layout lets it stretch depends on the window size.

## Will X notice I hid things?

No. Pagic changes the page in your browser only; nothing is sent to X, and your account, likes and follows are unaffected. The X app on your phone stays as it is.

## Other ways

There are X-only extensions that clean up the interface with switches, and feed blockers like NewsFeed Eradicator remove the whole timeline. They're fine if X is the only site you want to change.
