# How to Hide the LinkedIn Feed (and Keep Messages and Jobs)

To hide the LinkedIn feed on desktop, use a browser extension, because LinkedIn has no setting to turn it off. A feed blocker like NewsFeed Eradicator removes the whole feed in one switch. Pagic lets you choose: hide the feed, or keep it and hide only promoted posts, the news box, Puzzles and suggestions, while messages, jobs, search and profiles keep working.

## Can you turn off the LinkedIn feed in settings?

No. LinkedIn lets you tune the feed (unfollow people, "I don't want to see this", feed preferences), but there's no off switch. The home page always opens on the feed.

## Option 1: Hide the whole feed with Pagic

1. Open linkedin.com, click Pagic's icon and **Customize this site**.
2. Click the eraser (Hide).
3. Point at the feed in the middle. Press <kbd>↑</kbd> until the whole feed column is outlined, not one post, and click.
4. Hide the right column too, if you like (news, Puzzles, "Add to your feed").

Now the home page is your profile card, the post box and nothing to scroll. Messaging, My Network, Jobs and Notifications work as usual.

## Option 2: Keep the feed, lose the noise

If you use LinkedIn to post or keep up with a few people, hiding the feed is too much. Hide the parts you skip instead:

- **Promoted posts.** In Hide mode, point at a post labeled Promoted and <kbd>Alt</kbd>-click to hide all like it. Check the outline first: it should mark only the promoted ones. If it marks every post, hide promoted posts one at a time instead.
- **Suggested posts** and "Recommended for you" blocks, the same way.
- **The news and Puzzles box** in the right column.
- **"People you may know"** carousels.

Everything you hide is listed in Pagic's **Changes**, so you can bring any of it back.

Not through Pagic. It changes the page in your browser only and sends nothing to LinkedIn. Your profile, posts and connections are untouched, and the LinkedIn app on your phone is unaffected.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I stop seeing the LinkedIn feed?** LinkedIn has no setting for it. Use an extension: Pagic to hide the feed column, or a feed blocker like NewsFeed Eradicator.
**Can I hide promoted posts on LinkedIn?** Yes. In Pagic's Hide mode, Alt-click a promoted post to hide all like it, after checking the outline only marks promoted posts.
**Will messages and jobs still work?** Yes. Hiding the feed only changes the home page's middle column. Messaging, Jobs, My Network and search work as usual.
**Does this work in the LinkedIn mobile app?** No. Browser extensions work in desktop browsers like Chrome, Edge and Brave.
**Can I get the feed back for a day?** Yes. Switch the change off in Pagic's Changes list, or turn Pagic off for the site with the switch at the top of its panel.
