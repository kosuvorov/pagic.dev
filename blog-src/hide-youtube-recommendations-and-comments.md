# How to Hide YouTube Recommendations, Comments and the Home Feed

To hide YouTube recommendations and comments on desktop, use an extension that hides parts of the page and remembers it. YouTube only lets you pause watch history, which empties the home feed but not the sidebar, comments or Shorts. With Pagic you click each part (the home feed, the Up next sidebar, the comments, the end screens) and choose **Hide**, once. Here's the setup that turns YouTube into a search box with videos.

## What can you turn off in YouTube's own settings?

Not much on desktop:

- **Pause watch history** (YouTube → Settings → Manage all history). With history off and empty, YouTube stops filling the home page with recommendations. But the Up next sidebar, comments, Shorts and end screens stay.
- **Autoplay** has its own switch on the video player.
- **"Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel"** tune recommendations one video at a time.

Everything else needs to be hidden in the browser.

## The calm YouTube setup, step by step

Open YouTube, click Pagic's icon and **Customize this site**. Then click the eraser to open **Edit page** with Hide picked, and work through these:

1. **The home feed.** Point at the grid of videos on the home page. Press <kbd>↑</kbd> until the whole grid is outlined, not one video, and click. Home is now just the search box and the sidebar.
2. **Shorts.** <kbd>Alt</kbd>-click a Shorts shelf to hide every Shorts shelf, and hide the Shorts link in the left sidebar. More detail in [how to hide YouTube Shorts](/blog/how-to-hide-youtube-shorts/).
3. **Up next.** Open any video. Point at the list of recommendations on the right and hide the whole column. The video stays where it is.
4. **Comments.** Scroll down and hide the comments section. Or, if you like reading comments sometimes, use **Style → Blur until hover** instead: they stay blurred until you point at them.
5. **End screens.** The cards and video tiles that cover the last seconds of a video can be hidden the same way when they appear. Pause near the end, then click one.
6. **Explore, Trending, and the rest of the sidebar.** Hide whichever sections you never use.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> when you're done. All of it comes back hidden on your next visit.

## Can I hide recommendations only on video pages?

Yes, with Pagic Pro. A preset can apply only to pages whose address starts with something, like `/watch`. So you could keep recommendations on the home page and hide them while you watch, or the other way around. On Free, your changes apply to the whole site.

## What about a dedicated YouTube extension?

Unhook is the popular one: it has switches for the home feed, related videos, comments, Shorts, end screens and more. If YouTube is the only site you want calmer, it's quick to set up.

The reason to use Pagic instead is when YouTube isn't the only site. The same click-and-hide works on LinkedIn, X, Reddit and news sites, and it also does dark mode, fonts and notes. On Pagic's free plan, YouTube can be your one site.

## Will YouTube know I hid things?

No. Pagic only changes how the page looks in your browser. Nothing is sent to YouTube, and your account, history and recommendations elsewhere (on your phone or TV) are unaffected.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I turn off recommendations on YouTube?** On desktop, pausing and clearing watch history empties the home feed. To also hide the Up next sidebar, comments and Shorts, use an extension like Pagic or Unhook.
**Can I hide YouTube comments?** Yes. In Pagic, hide the comments section, or use Style → Blur until hover so comments stay blurred until you point at them.
**How do I get rid of the YouTube home feed?** Hide the video grid on the home page with Pagic, or pause and clear your watch history in YouTube's settings.
**Does hiding recommendations affect what YouTube recommends?** No. Hiding happens in your browser only; YouTube still sees what you watch as usual.
**Can I undo it?** Yes. Every hidden part is listed in Pagic's Changes, where you can switch it off or undo it.
