# How to Blur Spoilers, Thumbnails and Comments on Any Website

To blur spoilers on a website, use an extension that blurs the parts you choose and keeps them blurred on every visit. In Pagic, open **Edit page**, click the element, choose **Style** and turn on **Blur until hover**: it stays blurred until you point at it. It works on match scores, episode thumbnails, comment sections, or anything else you'd rather not see by accident.

## Why blur instead of hide?

Hiding removes something completely. Blurring keeps it there, so you can still find it and peek when you're ready:

- **Sports scores** you want to see after you've watched the match.
- **Episode titles and thumbnails** on a streaming or video site, when the title is the spoiler.
- **Comments** that are sometimes useful and often not.
- **Prices, numbers or names** on screen when you're sharing it.

## How to blur part of a page with Pagic

1. Open the site, click Pagic's icon and **Customize this site**.
2. Click **Edit page** and click the element to blur (a score box, a thumbnail, the comments).
3. Choose **Style**.
4. Turn on **Blur until hover** for spoilers, or **Blur** to keep it blurred even when you point at it.
5. Under **Hide as**, pick how it's hidden:
   - **Blur**: soft, like frosted glass.
   - **Pixelate**: squares, like a censored photo.
   - **Solid**: smeared into its own colors, so not even its shapes show. Useful for charts, where the shape of a line is the spoiler.
6. **Amount** sets how strong the blur is, or how big the squares are.

It's saved for the site. Next visit, it's blurred before you see it.

## Blur every thumbnail, not just one

Thumbnails repeat. Use <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click to pick several, or **Select all like this** from the Wand menu to pick every item of that kind, then **Style N** to blur them all alike. On YouTube, for example, that's how you blur every video thumbnail on the page, if thumbnails are what pull you into watching.

## Other blur tools

There are spoiler-blocker extensions that blur posts mentioning keywords you list, like a show's name. They're the better choice when spoilers can appear anywhere on a page (a social feed), because they read the text. Pagic blurs the *place* on the page you chose, which is better when the spoiler is always in the same spot, like a scoreboard or an episode list.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I blur spoilers in Chrome?** Use an extension. In Pagic, open Edit page, click the element, choose Style and turn on Blur until hover. It stays blurred until you point at it.
**Can I blur YouTube thumbnails?** Yes. Select all thumbnails with Select all like this, then Style them with Blur or Pixelate.
**What's the difference between blur, pixelate and solid?** Blur is soft, Pixelate turns it into squares, and Solid smears it into its own colors so even shapes don't show.
**Does blurring hide it from people watching my screen?** Yes, while it's blurred. It unblurs when you point at it if you chose Blur until hover, so use plain Blur for screen sharing.
**Can I unblur it later?** Yes. Switch the change off in Pagic's Changes list, or undo it.
