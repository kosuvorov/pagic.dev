Purchase terms

What you get with Pagic, how Pro billing works, and where to get help.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who provides Pagic

Pagic is developed and supplied by Kanstantsin Suvorau. For product, billing or privacy questions, contact support@pagic.dev. Polar acts as the reseller and merchant of record for Pro purchases, as described below.

Free, trial and Pro

Every new installation starts a 7-day Pro trial. No payment details are needed for the trial, and it does not automatically become a paid subscription. After it ends, Free lets you customize one site at a time; Reader remains free on supported websites.

Pro supports customization across sites and preset features such as copying, sharing and importing. Trial and Pro presets are kept when Pro ends, with the limits described on the pricing section and Pro page in the extension.

Prices and payment

Pro is offered at US$4.99 per month, US$24.99 per year, or US$49.99 for a lifetime license. Applicable taxes and the final total are shown at checkout. The monthly and yearly plans are recurring subscriptions. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase of a Pro license with no recurring subscription payment.

Polar acts as the reseller and merchant of record. Purchases are subject to the information shown at checkout and Polar's Checkout Buyer Terms. Save your receipt and review the price, billing period and any checkout conditions before purchasing.

Delivery and activation

After purchasing, find your license key in your purchase email or Polar customer portal. Open Pagic's Pro page and paste the key to activate this browser. An internet connection is needed for activation and periodic subscription checks. Any activation limit is shown with your license in Polar.

Removing a license from one browser releases that browser's activation where the license has an activation limit. Keep license keys private. Contact support if delivery or activation fails.

Renewal and cancellation

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. You can manage or cancel them in the Polar customer portal, also linked from Pagic's Pro page. The portal shows the effective cancellation date and paid-through period.

Uninstalling Pagic or removing its license key does not cancel billing. Canceling stops future renewals; it does not itself issue a refund. At the end of paid access, Pagic returns to Free and keeps saved presets under Free's limits.

Refunds and consumer rights

For a refund request, email support@pagic.dev with your order reference and a description of the issue, or use the support options on your Polar receipt. Refunds are processed through Polar and assessed under applicable law, Polar's Buyer Terms, and any product-specific refund terms shown at checkout.

This page does not exclude or limit mandatory consumer rights, including applicable rights concerning withdrawal, faulty digital content or refunds. A cancellation, trial or license activation does not by itself waive those rights.

Compatibility and optional AI

Pagic works on supported Chromium browsers and regular websites you authorize. Browser settings, extension stores and some other protected pages cannot be changed. Websites and browser support can change, so a particular page edit may need adjustment.

AI is optional. You provide your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, and the provider bills you separately. Pro does not include API credits. Review the privacy policy before sending page content or screenshots to an AI provider.

Your changes and support

Pagic changes how pages appear in your own browser. It does not publish your edits to the original website. Export important presets before removing the extension, and use page edits and shared content only where you have the necessary rights.

For product, billing or privacy questions, email support@pagic.dev. Polar's checkout and receipt identify the seller for your purchase.
