# Pagic

> Pagic is a Chrome extension that lets you customize any website without code: hide anything with a click (ads, feeds, YouTube Shorts, sidebars, cookie banners), recolor a site or give it dark mode, change its fonts, highlight text and leave sticky notes, and read articles in a clean Reader. Changes are saved per site and come back on every visit. It works in Chrome and Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc, Helium and others built on Chromium 127 or newer). Made by indie developer Konstantin Suvorov.

Key facts:

- A no-code alternative to Arc Boosts for Chrome: per-site colors, fonts, hiding elements ("zaps") and custom CSS, saved as presets.
- Hide: click any element to hide it on every visit; "Hide all like this" also hides matching items the site adds later, like new posts in a feed or Shorts shelves.
- Colors: a two-dot color pad tints the page and changes its accent; dark mode for sites that have none (with an Auto mode that follows the system); photos and videos keep their real colors; Google Docs and Sheets get a proper dark page.
- Text: any installed font or one from a file (including OpenDyslexic), line and letter spacing, text width.
- Markup: highlighter, underline, black marker, pen, arrows, shapes, sticky notes, spotlight and laser pointer; highlights and notes stay on the page on later visits.
- Reader: a clean reading view for articles, free on every site, opened with Alt+Shift+R.
- Edit page: rewrite text, move, resize, restyle, blur (or blur until hover) any element, swap pictures; every change is listed and can be switched off or undone.
- Optional AI: describe a change in words, using your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key.
- Privacy: no account; presets, notes and pictures are stored in the browser; Pagic asks for access to one site at a time.
- Pricing model: Free customizes one site at a time with every tool, and Reader is free everywhere. Pro customizes every site and adds presets (several looks per site, looks for some pages or for dark mode, export and import), with monthly, yearly and one-time lifetime options.
