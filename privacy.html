Privacy

Short version: what you do with Pagic stays in your browser.

What Pagic stores

Your presets, hidden elements, notes, drawings, pictures, fonts and settings are saved in your browser's own extension storage, on your computer. Pagic has no account and no server, so none of it is sent to us.

Which sites Pagic can see

Pagic asks for access to a site the first time you customize it, one site at a time. On sites you haven't allowed, it does nothing unless you open it there yourself (clicking its icon or opening Reader), and then only in that tab.

The AI helper

The AI helper in Advanced mode is optional and off until you add your own API key for Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI). When you ask it for a change, Pagic sends your request, a description of the page and, if needed, a screenshot of the tab straight from your browser to the provider you chose, under their privacy policy. Your key stays in your browser. Nothing is sent when you don't use it.

Tracking

The extension has no analytics, ads or tracking. This website may count visits with Umami, which doesn't use cookies or collect personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
